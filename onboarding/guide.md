## 2. Words you will see

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Website** | Pages people read on their phone or computer. Yours will open in नेपाली, with an English version. |
| **Web address** (also called **domain**) | The name people type to reach your website: **{domain}**. It works like a house address. |
| **DNS** | A public directory that turns your web address into the location of the computer where your website lives, like a phone book. We set it up for you. |
| **Server** (also called **hosting**) | The computer, always switched on, where your website lives. We look after it. |
| **Page** | One screen of the website, such as "About us". |
| **Menu** | The list of sections at the top of every page. |
| **Footer** | The strip at the bottom of every page, with your address, registration details and links. |
| **Banner** | The large photo at the top of the home page, with your mission on it. |
| **Form** | Boxes a visitor fills in and sends to you, such as their name and phone number. |
| **Appeal** | A request for money for one clear need, such as "school fees for 20 children", with a target amount. |
| **Pledge** | A supporter's promise to give an amount, which your team then arranges with them. |
| **Consent** | Permission. Before we show a person's photo or story, they (or, for a child, a parent or guardian) must agree. |
| **Admin** | The private part of the website where your team writes and changes pages. Only people with a password can open it. |

## 3. How your website is arranged

Every charity's website on our platform is arranged the same way. A supporter who has used one finds
everything in the same place on the next, and donors find what they look for first: what you do,
who you are, and where their money goes. Your website still looks like your organisation, through
your logo, colours, words and photos.

### The top of every page

Your logo and name on the left (they lead back to the home page), the **Donate** button on the
right, the language switch (नेपाली / English), and the menu. On a phone, the menu opens from a
**Menu** button.

### Your menu

The menu has the same sections, in the same order and with the same names, on every charity's
website:

| Section | नेपाली | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **About us** | हाम्रो बारेमा | What you do and where, who runs your organisation, your registration, and **Reports and accounts** (annual reports and audited accounts) |
| **Appeals** | सहयोग अपिल | Your open appeals and how much each has raised, then past appeals |
| **Stories** | कथाहरू | News and stories about your work |
| **Get involved** | सहभागी हुनुहोस् | Volunteering, raising money for you, partnering, giving goods or time |
| **Contact** | सम्पर्क | Your address, phone, email, a map link and a message form |
| **Donate** (the button) | दान दिनुहोस् | Every way to give, what a gift does, and where the money goes |

You cannot rename, reorder or remove these sections, but you **can add your own pages**, either after
them or inside one of them (for example a page about your school inside About us).

### The bottom of every page (footer)

Your organisation's name; your **registration line** (District Administration Office registration
number and district, Social Welfare Council affiliation number and PAN); your address, phone and
email; your social media links; and links to Reports and accounts, the privacy notice, the
accessibility statement and Contact.

### Pages we write for you

- **Privacy notice:** how you use the details people give you through the website's forms. We write
  it from your answers in section 4.6.
- **Accessibility statement:** how the website works for people with disabilities, and who to
  contact if something doesn't work for them.

## 4. Step 1: what we need to go live

### 4.1 Home page

as every charity's home page, in this order:

A part with nothing in it yet (for example appeals, before you have any) is simply not shown.

**What to send:**

| Item | What to send | Length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Your mission** | One sentence that says what you do and for whom | Up to 20 words |
| **Banner photo** | One photo that shows your work (see the photo rules in 4.8) | 1 photo |

If you are not sure about the mission sentence, send us a few ideas and we will help you choose.

### 4.2 About us

| Item | What to send | Length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Who we are** | Who you are, where you work, and who you help | 1–3 short paragraphs |
| **Our story** | When and why you started, and what has changed since | 1–3 short paragraphs |
| **Our vision** | The future you are working towards | 1–2 sentences |
| **Numbers** | See 4.3 | Up to 4 |
| **Who runs the organisation** | The names and roles of your committee members and staff, only for people who agree to be shown | — |
| **Reports and accounts** | Your annual reports and audited accounts for the last 1–3 years, if you have them | Files (PDF or photos) |
| **Photos** (optional) | 1–3 photos of your centre, classes or activities | 1–3 photos |
| **A quote** (optional) | A short quote from a parent, teacher or volunteer, with their name or role, if they agree | 1–3 sentences |

**Why reports and accounts matter.** Donors and organisations that fund charities look for them
first. Showing them is one of the strongest signs that a charity can be trusted.

### 4.3 Numbers (statistics)

Numbers help people trust you and remember you. About us shows up to 4 numbers in a row, each with a
short label. For each number, please send:

- the **number** and a **short label**, for example "120 · children in school";
- **since when** it counts, for example "since 2070 BS" or "this year";
- **where it comes from**, for example your yearly report, so you can answer if someone asks.

Please only send numbers you are sure are true. A wrong number on a website is hard to take back.

### 4.4 Contact page

- Your **address**, as you want it written in नेपाली and in English.
- A **link to your office on Google Maps** (open Google Maps, find your office, press Share, copy the
  link).
- A **phone number** to show, if you want one.
- An **email address** to show. An email address at your own web address (like info@{domain}) can be
  set up after the web address is approved; it is a separate step and may have a small monthly cost,
  so we will talk to you first. Until then we can show an email address you already use, or none.
- Your **Facebook page**, and links to any other social media you use (Instagram, YouTube, TikTok).

The Contact page also has a message form, so people can write to you without an email program.

### 4.5 Your registration details

Every charity's website shows the same registration line in its footer, because supporters and
donors check it. Please send:

- your **District Administration Office registration number** and the district;
- your **Social Welfare Council affiliation number**, if you have one;
- your **PAN** (Permanent Account Number).

### 4.6 Privacy

The website's forms (pledges, volunteering, messages) collect names, phone numbers and email
addresses, so the website must say how you use them. We write the privacy notice for you; please
tell us:

- Who at your organisation looks after supporters' details?
- How long will you keep them?
- Who can a supporter contact to ask you to delete their details?

### 4.7 Get involved

Which ways can people help you: volunteering, raising money for you, partnering with you (for
organisations), or giving goods or time? We show only the ones you choose. If you want volunteers,
tell us what to ask them (for example name, phone, when they are free, how they can help).

If organisations support you regularly and agree to show their logo, send us their names and logos:
they appear near the bottom of the home page.

### 4.8 Your logo, colours and photos

**Logo and colours.** Send your logo if you have one. The website's colours can match your logo, or
you can tell us the colours you want. You can also choose a light or a dark strip at the top and
bottom of every page. We check that every colour combination is easy to read for everyone,
including people with weak eyesight.

**Photo rules:**

- **Phone photos are fine.** Send the original photo (not a screenshot), in good light, not blurry.
- **Send photos through the form, or as files.** On WhatsApp, send them as a "Document" so they are
  not made smaller; Google Drive also works.
- **Banner photo:** taken wide (phone held sideways), with the important part in the middle. On
  phones the website shows only the middle of the photo.
- **For each photo, tell us** who took it (we show their name under it) and whether everyone in it
  agreed to be shown (section 9).

## 5. Step 2: Stories (start now)

Stories are the best way to show what your work changes. One good story is better than five rushed
ones. We publish each story when it is ready, so please start now and send them one at a time. One
or two stories a month is a good pace.

**For each story, please send:**

| Item | Length |
| --- | --- |
| A **title** | Up to 10 words |
| A **short summary**, shown in the list of stories | 1–2 sentences |
| The **story** | About 300–800 words |
| 1–3 **photos** | See 4.8 and 9 |
| The **date** it happened | — |

**Ideas for stories:** a child's first year at school; someone who got a wheelchair or hearing aid; a
parent's or teacher's experience; an event or a training; a thank-you to a donor who agrees to be
named. The thank-you notes you already share on Facebook can be stories too.

Stories can be in नेपाली only at first; an English version can follow later. Later, your team can add
stories yourselves in the admin. We will show you how, and help until you are comfortable.

## 6. Step 3: Appeals and Donate (prepare now)

### 6.1 Appeals

Please think now about **2–3 appeals**, so we can publish them as soon as online giving opens.

**For each appeal, please send:**

| Item | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **What it is for** | School fees and books for 20 children |
| **Why it matters**: a short explanation of the need | 2–3 paragraphs |
| **A summary**, shown on the home page and the list of appeals | Up to 2 sentences |
| **A photo** | 1 photo |
| **A target amount**, if you have one | Rs 2,00,000 |
| **An end date**, or "ongoing" | 30 Chaitra 2083, or ongoing |
| **Up to 4 suggested amounts**, each with what it pays for | Rs 500: books for one child for a year |

### 6.2 The Donate page

The Donate page shows every way to give, what a gift does, and where the money goes. Please tell us:

- **Which ways people can give you:** eSewa, Khalti, a QR code, bank transfer, or in person at your
  office.
- **Bank details to show,** if you take bank transfers: account name, bank, branch and account
  number. Only an account in your organisation's name.
- **Your QR codes,** if you have them for donations: the whole card as your app or bank gives it
  (a screenshot or saved image), for each app or bank. For each one we also need its **name** as
  supporters will pick it (for example "eSewa" or "Fonepay (bank)"), the **account name your app
  shows after scanning it**, exactly as written, and optionally the **account or wallet number**.
  Supporters scan the code, then tell us they have paid, and our team marks the gift received
  once it reaches your account. A QR code takes real money from the first day, with no merchant
  account.
- **Where the money goes.** Many donors ask how much of their gift reaches the people you help (for
  example "Rs 85 of every Rs 100"). We show this only if your audited accounts show it is true.
- **Tax relief.** If the Inland Revenue Department has approved your organisation for tax-exempt
  donations, the Donate page tells donors they can deduct their gift from their taxable income.
  Please tell us whether you are approved.
- **Your refund policy** (optional): whether and how you give a gift back, for example if someone
  pays twice by mistake. Supporters read it before they pay. If you haven't decided one, leave it
  out: the website then says nothing about refunds.

### 6.3 Online giving by eSewa and Khalti

The website can take gifts by eSewa and Khalti. To receive the money, your organisation needs a
**merchant account** with each one: an account for receiving payments, in the organisation's name.
We will guide you through it. **Never send us passwords or secret codes from these accounts in a
chat;** we will show you a safe way.

### 6.4 Thanking supporters

Later, the home page can thank recent supporters by name. Only supporters who tick "show my name"
when they give are ever shown.

## 8. Your web address: {domain}

Web addresses ending in **.org.np** are for organisations registered in Nepal, and they are free.
They are approved by the organisation that manages addresses in Nepal, which can take some days.

- **What we do:** apply, and connect the address to your website (the DNS step in section 2). The
  website goes live at this address as soon as it is approved.
- **What you may need to do:** the approval office checks that the address matches the name on your
  registration certificate, and may ask for a copy of the certificate or a request letter on your
  letterhead. If they ask, we will tell you exactly what to send.
- **Who owns it:** the address belongs to your organisation, not to us.

## 9. Photos and stories of people: consent

Your website will be public. Anyone can see it, copy photos and share them. The people you work
with, and children most of all, deserve extra care, so we follow these rules:

- **Written consent** from every adult shown, and from a parent or guardian for every child shown,
  in a photo or story. A simple signed note is enough; we can give you a short form in नेपाली.
- **First name only, or no name, for children.** We never show a child's full name, school or home
  village together with their photo.
- **Dignity first.** Choose photos that show people learning, playing or achieving, not suffering.
- **Anyone can change their mind.** If someone asks us to remove a photo or story, we remove it.
