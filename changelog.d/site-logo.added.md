Moderators can add the charity's logo in **Site settings → Brand → Logo**, and it shows beside
the charity's name at the top of every page, in both languages. A long name wraps beside it
instead of crowding the page, a site without a logo looks exactly as before, and the logo can be
previewed before it's saved
([#123](https://github.com/dipbazz/Charity-wagtail-CMS/issues/123)).
