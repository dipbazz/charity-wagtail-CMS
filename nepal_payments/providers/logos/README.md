# Providers' logos

Each provider's own logo, as the provider publishes it, unchanged: shown on its pay button so a
supporter finds their app at a glance (#201). Use them only to say a payment can be made with that
provider, and never recolour, stretch, crop, outline or add effects to them. Replace a file only
with a newer one from the same source, and update the table.

## eSewa's rules

From eSewa's [Logomark Guidelines](https://cdn.esewa.com.np/ui/notice/Logo_Guidelines.pdf):

- On white or a light background, the full-colour logo: green `#3cc850`, off-black `#111111`, and
  the inside of the "e" always white. On a photo, white only. Never all black.
- Keep clear space around it: about the width of two small "e"s.
- Never skew, stretch, rotate, recolour, outline, shadow or rearrange it.
- In text the name is **eSewa** (or **इसेवा** in Nepali), never "Esewa", "e-Sewa" or "ESEWA".

## Khalti

Khalti publishes no logo guide since the merger, so the same rules apply: unchanged, on white, with
space around it. Khalti's merchant terms ask merchants to show its logo where it can be paid with.
The "Khalti by IME" wordmark linked from khalti.com wasn't available (403) on 17 Oct 2026; check
for it when Khalti publishes brand assets.
